The opening of an experience is where intent matters most. The user hasn't decided to invest yet — every distraction is a reason to leave.

## Apply these together

- Show one thought per screen, with the next action obvious
- Reveal complexity progressively as commitment grows
- Make every interactive element large enough to hit confidently
- Reserve motion for communicating intent — never decoration

If your onboarding has more than three steps, the rule of *progressive disclosure* applies recursively to the steps themselves.
