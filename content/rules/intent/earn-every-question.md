# Earn Every Question

When a question is unexpected, unusual, or asks for effort the customer wasn't prepared to give, explain why it's being asked and what happens with the answer.

## Why
Unexplained questions create suspicion. A customer who doesn't understand why they're being asked something either guesses (bad data) or abandons (bad outcome).

## In practice
- **Drop-off time:** "Not sure of the exact time? Don't worry — most car parks allow you to arrive up to 3 hours either side of your booked drop-off time…"
- **Cruise question:** "From missed ports to cabin confinement, additional baggage cover and itinerary changes, our cruise cover helps with the extras."

Each explanation addresses the customer's most likely objection at that exact moment. It is not boilerplate.

## The test
What would a customer think if they saw this question with no explanation? If the honest answer involves confusion, doubt or a privacy concern — add a brief explanation.
