On every screen, exactly one action is the most likely next step. Design as if 95% of users will take it, because they will.

## What "obvious" means

- **Visual weight:** filled, accent-coloured, larger or bolder than anything else interactive on screen.
- **Position:** bottom-right on desktop; bottom of viewport, full-width on mobile.
- **Verb:** describes the outcome ("Save trip", "Book now"), not the mechanic ("Submit", "OK").
- **State:** disabled state has a tooltip explaining what's missing — never silently grey.

## When you have *two* primaries

You don't. You have one primary and one secondary. Secondary is a ghost button or a text link, never the same colour as primary.

## The blink test

Glance at the screen for 200ms, look away. What did you see? If the answer isn't *the primary CTA*, the hierarchy is broken — usually because something else (an illustration, a banner, a heading) is competing for the same visual weight.
