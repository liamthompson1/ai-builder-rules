# Show What You've Heard

When a question depends on a previous answer, show that answer on the current screen. Don't make the customer remember what they told you.

## Why
Memory load is friction. If a customer is selecting a return time but can't remember what departure date they chose, they either abandon to check or guess. Both are bad.

## In practice
- The "Return date" screen shows a context strip: `Dropping off: Wed 13 May 2026 at 06:00`.
- The "Car collection time" question embeds the selected return date directly in the question text: `What time will you collect your car on Wed 20 May 2026?`

## The test
Could the customer give an answer on this screen that contradicts something they said on a previous screen? If yes — show the prior answer on this screen.
