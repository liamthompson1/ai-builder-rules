# Speak The Customer's Language

Question text is written the way a customer would say it, not the way a system stores it.

## Why
When a customer sees "Select departure airport" they experience a database query. When they see "Flying from" they experience a conversation.

## Examples (good vs bad)
| Good (conversational) | Bad (database) |
|---|---|
| "Flying from" | "Select departure airport" |
| "What time will you drop your car off?" | "Enter drop-off time" |
| "Going on a cruise?" | "Add cruise cover supplement" |
| "Returning to collect car" | "Return date" |
| "Where are you staying?" | "Enter accommodation details" |

## The test
Read the question aloud. Would a person say it that way? If it sounds like it came from a database schema — rewrite it.
