# Summarise Before You Reveal

Before showing results, show the customer a summary of everything they've told you. Give them a chance to check, amend, and then commit to seeing results.

## Why
Results without context feel arbitrary. A summary step:
- Gives the customer confidence that the results reflect their actual situation
- Creates a checkpoint where errors can be corrected
- Builds the sense that the results have been curated specifically for them

## In practice
All three wizards (Parking, Insurance, Transfers) end with a "Search Summary" step before the results CTA.

- Each summary row has an edit icon.
- The CTA ("Show prices and availability", "Show transfers") appears **below** the summary — never before it.
- A nudge card below the CTA provides a final confidence signal.

## The test
Could a customer arrive at the results page having misremembered one of their earlier answers? If yes — add a summary step.
